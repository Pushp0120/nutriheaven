Run by hand, not by scale

Nutri Heaven started as one shop in Bishnoi Colony with a simple idea: sell dry fruits the way you'd want them sold to your own family. Pardeep Sheoran sources and checks stock in person, batch by batch, rather than buying in bulk from wherever is cheapest.

It stays small on purpose. That's what lets us tell you honestly what's fresh this week, and what to skip until the next one.
